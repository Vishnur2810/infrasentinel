Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to every InfraSentinel server run by your organization or by us.

Written to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). It is a starting template: have it checked by a lawyer before a commercial launch.

What we collect

  • Your account: email address, organization name and type, a hashed password (we never store the password itself), and your 2-step sign-in key if you turn it on.
  • From each machine you connect: its name, operating system, IP addresses, which network services are listening, security settings (for example firewall on/off, update status, administrator accounts) and the problems found.
  • Log summaries (unless you use --no-logs): counts of failed and successful sign-ins, the addresses they came from, the usernames tried, new accounts, and up to 15 example log lines.
  • Other devices on your network (unless you use --no-network): their local IP and hardware (MAC) addresses, as seen by the machine you connected.
  • Questions you ask the AI, while the conversation is open in your browser.

What we never collect

  • The contents of your files, documents, emails, databases, patient records or student records.
  • Passwords from your machines. The checker only reads whether settings are safe.

Why we use it

Only to show you security problems, explain them, and check whether fixes worked. We don't sell data, show ads or use your data to train AI models.

Where it is stored

  • In a single database on the InfraSentinel server (data/infrasentinel.db), readable only by the server's user. It can be encrypted at rest (IS_ENCRYPT=1).
  • The AI model runs on the InfraSentinel server itself; questions aren't sent to an AI company.
  • If you switch on Search the web, a short search phrase (the problem title and your OS name, never your IPs or hostnames) is sent to DuckDuckGo, or to the search service the server owner configured.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. Each machine keeps only its latest report plus a short history (the last 30 checks and 50 verified fixes). Removing a machine deletes its data. Deleting your account deletes everything at once.

Your rights

  • See and download all your data: Account & security → Download my data.
  • Correct it by re-running the checker, or delete it: Account & security → Delete my account.
  • Withdraw consent any time by uninstalling the checker (--uninstall) and deleting the account.
  • Questions or complaints: contact the organization that runs your InfraSentinel server. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2-SHA256, 200,000 rounds). Optional 2-step sign-in. Sign-in attempts are rate-limited. The checker is read-only, connects out to the dashboard only, and can be verified against a published SHA-256 fingerprint.
