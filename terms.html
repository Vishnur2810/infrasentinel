Terms of use

Last updated: October 2026

A starting template for a student prototype. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before any commercial use.

1. What InfraSentinel is

InfraSentinel is an advisory tool. It looks at the settings of machines you connect and explains possible security problems in plain language. It is not a guarantee that your systems are safe, a penetration test, or a certified security audit.

2. Only check machines you are allowed to

Connect only machines that you own or that you have written permission to check. You are responsible for that permission.

3. AI explanations

Explanations and fix steps written by the AI can be wrong. Steps marked Checked steps are written and reviewed by people; AI answers are labelled as AI-written. Read each step before you run it, keep a backup, and keep a second way into the machine when you change sign-in, firewall or remote-access settings. For anything marked Call a professional, use a qualified security professional.

4. Responsibility

InfraSentinel helps you find and prioritise problems, but you decide what to change. To the extent the law allows, InfraSentinel and its makers are not liable for losses caused by security incidents, missed problems, or changes you make based on its advice. Nothing here limits rights you have under consumer protection law.

5. Reporting incidents

If you think you've been attacked, contact a security professional. In India, certain cyber security incidents must be reported to CERT-In (cert-in.org.in) within 6 hours of noticing them. Your professional can tell you whether this applies.

6. Plans and payment

Prices shown in the app are samples. No payment is taken in this version.

7. Your account

Keep your password and authenticator app safe. You can download or delete your data at any time from Account & security.
